How it works

Three moves that turn policy memos into chairside action across the whole network.

  1. 01

    Set

    Write the standard once in plain language — FMX cadence, fluoride at prophy, your rules — and it covers every location instantly.

  2. 02

    Prove

    Backtest against last year’s patients and forecast forward before anyone changes a chairside habit.

  3. 03

    Push

    Land the standard in morning huddles and daily worklists the same day — communicated, not commanded.
